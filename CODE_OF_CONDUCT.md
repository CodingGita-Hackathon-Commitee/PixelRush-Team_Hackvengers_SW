# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the SurplusLink project welcoming
and respectful for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, respectful, and constructive.
- Share feedback about ideas and code, not personal attributes.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Respect privacy and do not share another person's personal information
  without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, and publishing private
information without consent are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer through the
contact method provided by the repository owner. Please include enough
context to help the maintainers understand what happened. Reports will be
handled as discreetly as reasonably possible.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and
may take appropriate action in response to behavior that violates them.
